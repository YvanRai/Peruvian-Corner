Privacy Policy

What information may be collected when you visit this site, and how to reach us with any questions.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Who we are

This site is operated by Peruvian Corner LLC, a restaurant located at 67-456 Goodale Ave, Waialua, HI 96791. For any questions about this policy, you can reach us at peruviancorner808@gmail.com.

What information we collect

This site has no forms of its own and does not collect personal data directly. That said, a few things are worth knowing:

  • Loading the page connects to external services (Google Fonts for typefaces, Google Maps for the embedded map) that may log your IP address under their own privacy policies.
  • If you leave a review through our Google Forms link, Google may automatically record the email address tied to the Google account you use to fill it out — that's handled by Google, we only receive your response.
  • We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or our own analytics tools.

Links to third-party services

The site links out to WhatsApp, Instagram, Yelp, and our online ordering platform (Toast). Each of these services has its own privacy policy, separate from ours — we recommend reviewing it if you share information there, for example when placing an order or messaging us on WhatsApp.

How we use information

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. If you message us on WhatsApp, email, or through the review form, we use that information only to respond to you or handle your order or inquiry.

Your rights

You can reach us anytime at peruviancorner808@gmail.com with questions about this policy, or if you'd like us to delete any information you've shared with us directly (for example, your WhatsApp number or email).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes to the site or the services we use. The date of the last update appears above.
